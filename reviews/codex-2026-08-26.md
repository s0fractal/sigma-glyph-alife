**Verdict:** **CHANGES REQUESTED**

This is a review of the claims and their executable support, not a request to
rewrite a null result into a positive one. The repository is unusually candid
about failed hypotheses and underdetermined preregistrations. The current
flagship result nevertheless contains one quantitative misstatement, two
identification failures, and a release-gate hole large enough that the headline
experiment is absent from the advertised full matrix.

## Findings

### [BLOCKER] The claimed 0.8%–7.0% ATP share has the wrong denominator and is not the currency difference

`experiments/alife-exp-010/measure.py:322-329` appends a cost only when a reaction
settles successfully. The report then computes
`ok * mean_cost` at `measure.py:881-887` and labels it “ATP spent”. The numerator,
`rs_atp`, includes every fired R-S action, including actions in reactions that
later starve or park. The denominator excludes those reactions. The ledger does
track all spending (`measure.py:369-375`), but the receipt does not expose the
terminal `spent` and `held` quantities needed by the result.

This is not a cosmetic discrepancy. In Arm E there are no terminal holds, so
actual total spend is exactly `endowment - pool_left`. Recomputing from the frozen
receipt changes the stated R-S shares:

| seed | reported | R-S / actual total spend |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 20260825 | 2.79% | 1.23% |
| 20260826 | 1.94% | 0.62% |
| 20260827 | 6.97% | 2.04% |

For Arm M, terminal agents hold ATP that is not emitted, so the current receipt
only permits intervals: 0.58%–1.60%, 0.37%–1.50%, and 0.33%–0.79%. The published
single values are the upper ends, not identified shares of all colony spend.

More importantly, total R-S spend is not “the whole difference between the two
currencies” (`RESULT.md:140-146`). The price intervention is the counterfactual
saving `book_I_cost - floor_cost`, not `book_I_cost`. On the identical Arm E
traces, repricing each R-S at the floor gives savings of 0.77%, 0.40%, and 1.39%
of actual spend. Comparing observed E and M totals cannot recover that estimand
because the arms take different trajectories.

**Required correction:** emit `spent_total`, terminal held ATP, successful spend,
failed spend, parked spend, and the per-event Book-I/floor counterfactual pair.
Replace the headline interval with the correctly named estimand. Do not infer a
cross-arm currency effect from an R-S expenditure share.

### [BLOCKER] ALIFE-EXP-010 is not run by the repository's full test command or CI

Neither `tools/test-all.sh` nor either workflow under `.github/workflows/` invokes
ALIFE-EXP-010's controls, replay, or validation. `test-all.sh` reaches EXP-008 and
then receipt/proof guards; EXP-010 appears only as static receipt material seen by
generic guards. Thus the latest headline can rot while the advertised matrix is
green. A digest/shape guard is not a replay of the experiment-specific controls.

The ordinary local invocation is also deliberately non-terminal: it skips the
slow EXP-007/008 replays and the external Decision Archaeology validator. That is
properly printed as `NOT COMPLETE`, but makes a bare “test-all is green” claim
false unless `RUN_SLOW=1` and the validator path are supplied.

**Required correction:** add EXP-010 controls, replay, validation, and receipt
identity to the slow/full path and to CI with relevant path triggers. Add a
negative mutation that changes an EXP-010 scored field while leaving generic JSON
shape valid. Document one canonical command that is terminal only when every
currently claimed experiment has actually replayed.

### [MAJOR] The two arms are a compound intervention, so they do not identify “currency chooses colony”

Arm M changes at least four mechanisms together: the ATP price, affordability,
the requirement for a living exact-hash victim, and removal of that victim. It
also introduces blocking and wake-up dynamics. Subsequent random choices diverge
because culling and other state-dependent calls consume different positions in
the RNG stream. The result itself admits that the hard constraint and RNG
divergence dominate (`RESULT.md:148-154`), but still frames the experiment as a
test between currencies.

H1 therefore establishes only that two compound transition kernels produce
different terminal samples. Its post-hoc same-arm baseline correctly shows that
the preregistered Jaccard threshold has no discriminative power. H2 and H3 cannot
attribute their signs to price, matter conservation, or victim availability.

**Required successor:** a preregistered 2x2 factorial design:

1. Book-I price versus action-floor price;
2. copy freely available versus exact-hash body required and consumed.

Use counter-based randomness keyed by `(seed, reaction, event type)` so a cull in
one arm does not shift every later draw in that arm. Name the price main effect,
matter main effect, and interaction separately. If this design is infeasible,
the repository should narrow the claim to comparison of two reactor policies,
not currencies.

### [MAJOR] “Permanent” waiting and the H3 failure count are right-censoring artifacts

The run stops after reaction 1000. It does not continue the environment to show
that the 152/130/73 queued reactions can never receive their missing molecule.
Nevertheless `RESULT.md:198-201` says the copy “never arrived” and
`RESULT.md:254-257` calls the reactions permanently waiting. What is measured is
unresolved-at-horizon, not permanent non-arrival.

At `measure.py:426-427`, every outstanding wait is added to `fail`. This makes
Arm M's final-window success rate depend on follow-up time: later births have
less opportunity to wake, while Arm E has no corresponding censored state. The
preregistered H3 comparison is therefore confounded by arm-specific censoring.

**Required correction:** preserve `waiting` as a third outcome. After stopping
new births, either run a preregistered settlement phase, or analyse time to
settlement with right censoring and a fixed follow-up horizon. Reserve
“permanent” for a proved reachability exclusion or an exhaustive terminal-state
argument.

### [MAJOR] The proposed redundancy mechanism is not measured

`RESULT.md:96-101` explains higher distinctness as “eating a duplicate” and
spending redundancy rather than population. The gate only proves that one living
victim has the demanded hash. It does not record how many living bodies had that
hash immediately before consumption. The consumed victim can be the last living
copy; the `z` occurrence being duplicated is a subterm/address in the reaction,
not necessarily another census body.

No event-level mediation statistic connects pre-consumption multiplicity to the
change in distinct hashes. The observed diversity may instead result from less
culling, blocked reactions, or the diverged trajectory.

**Required correction:** record victim-hash multiplicity before and after every
consumption, whether the event removes the last body of that hash, the product's
novelty, and the counterfactual cull that would have occurred. Score the proposed
mechanism directly or label it a hypothesis for the successor.

### [MAJOR] The receipt guard can let an under-sampled null borrow an unrelated draw count

`tools/receipt_guard.py:42-56` accepts a named null whenever `has_draws(obj)` is
true for the entire containing dictionary. One unrelated subtree with
`draws: 20` therefore licenses every sibling null, including one with `draws: 1`.
A minimal reproducer is:

```sh
python3 - <<'PY'
from tools.receipt_guard import offenders
print(list(offenders({'null_a': {'draws': 1},
                      'unrelated': {'draws': 20}})))
PY
```

Current output is `[]`. The guard's stated locality rule is therefore not
enforced.

**Required correction:** bind a draw count to the named null value or to one
explicitly defined parent schema; never search arbitrary siblings. Add deletion,
undersampling, and unrelated-sibling negative mutations.

### [MODERATE] “Attractor” is not operationalised by this experiment

The frame is three 1000-reaction trajectories with no burn-in, stationarity
test, recurrence criterion, mixing diagnostic, or convergence across horizons.
Terminal survivor sets and L1 cores are finite-horizon states, not demonstrated
attractors. The repository is careful elsewhere to call this a bounded soup, but
the research question and interpretation still import attractor language.

**Required correction:** either define an operational attractor criterion before
the next run (recurrence basin, stationary distribution, or persistent motif
under increasing horizons), or consistently rename the outcome “finite-horizon
population state”.

### [MODERATE] The census inequality is not yet a matter-conservation law

The checked bound at `measure.py:376-380` sums `s0` over every individual ever
born, including dead and culled individuals. Its right-hand side can only grow
and can become arbitrarily loose. The local “material shortfall” check is also
largely structural: an exact-hash victim has the same represented term as `z`, so
its size matches by construction. Meanwhile the content-addressed store retains
consumed terms; “freed matter” is a population-census metaphor, not storage
deallocation.

This is acceptable as a checked census invariant, but not evidence that the
implementation conserves a physical resource. A stronger version needs an
explicit stock-flow ledger with production, consumption, culling, retained
storage, and garbage collection named separately.

## Scientific disposition

The negative hypothesis outcomes can stand. The numerical headline and causal
story cannot. The strongest publishable current conclusion is narrower:

> In this bounded implementation, two compound reactor policies produce
> seed-sensitive finite-horizon populations; the preregistered survivor-overlap
> statistic is non-discriminating, and outstanding matter waits are substantial
> at the observation horizon. The marginal ATP price effect has not yet been
> identified.

That is a useful result. It points directly to a factorial successor and prevents
the repository from explaining an intervention it did not isolate.
